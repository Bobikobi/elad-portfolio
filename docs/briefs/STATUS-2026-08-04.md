## 2. Two production bugs, found and fixed on branches cut from master

Both were measured on the LIVE site, both are one concern each, and neither carries any of
the in-progress redesign.

### PR #26 - the canvas mounts on every route (launch blocker)

Measured on `www.eladsaadon.dev`:

| route | canvas | JS | blocking |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/services/nextjs-development` | yes | 658KB (421KB three.js) | **411ms** |
| `/guides/website-cost-guide` | yes | 658KB | 153ms |

Every text page downloads the 3D engine and blocks the main thread to render a paragraph.
`ClientProviders` always knew which routes are immersive; `CosmicStage` answered separately
and without the route. After: **236KB / 0ms**, 20 of 20 route x view-mode combinations
correct, cosmic routes unchanged. The same defect was also costing those pages their footer.

The test had to be a new one rather than a fix to `sectionForPath`, whose first-segment
behaviour is correct for the navbar's active state and the section metadata.

**One bug found while verifying it, from the same family:** `localeForPath` answers "which
locale does this route render in", not "does this path carry a locale prefix", and returns
`'en'` for an unprefixed English route. Slicing on it ate the `services` segment and put
the canvas straight back. Checked against fourteen route shapes.

### PR #27 - a visitor returning from a world cannot scroll back

Reported as "scrolling back breaks at some stages". Reproduced: after visiting a world and
returning home the document is **900px tall against a 900px viewport** - not scrollable at
all. The visitor is parked in the solar overview with no way back to the galaxy short of a
reload.

A deliberate decision doing more than it meant to: a returning visitor should not have the
intro replayed, so the 500vh driver was not rendered for them - and the runway went with
it, along with the scroll feed and the curtain's auto-commit. The driver is mounted for
both arrivals now and a returning visitor starts at the END of it.

Verified: returns at `prog 1` in the overview with no replay; scrolling up reaches
`act: galaxy`; **the fresh arrival is unchanged** (`y 0`, galaxy, dive intact).

Both PRs are open and unmerged. The plan says OWNER MERGES; the rollback deployment id is
recorded in #26.

## 3. B8d, finished

The owner's redesign of the projects windows, plus the two external-review defects it does
not dissolve. Full numbers in `B8d-verify.md`. In short:

- Windows carry the site preview and no painted text; the words appear on the planet for
  whichever project is hovered, or - with no pointer - the one at the centre of the fan.
  The copy stays in the DOM off-screen, because crawlers get the cosmic view by default.
- The ring lies in **Saturn's ring plane**: one matrix carries a canonical circular space to
  the screen, and the applied transform matches the basis the rig publishes to 2dp.
- Navbar collision fixed (highest window y=205 against a navbar ending at 64, was y=22);
  the header's zone is a 300px reservation rather than a floor on leftovers.
- Four owner corrections after that: openable projects first and non-opening last, a drawn
  monogram where there is no screenshot, words on hover only where a pointer exists, and
  windows that rise out of the ring instead of switching on.
- Mobile: the swipe follows the fan sideways, the panel is sized to the phone, and the
  preview got its opacity back after the caching refactor silently dropped it.

**Accepted deviation, ruled and not to be re-litigated:** three windows at ~56fps on the low
tier rather than 60. The caching fix the reviewer asked for went in first and changed
nothing (49.5-51.7 either side), no single visual feature accounts for it, and shrinking the
vector layer's raster area by 69% moved it not at all. What survives is compositing an
animated SVG layer over a 3D canvas.

**Four bugs caught during verification**, each of which would have shipped: mirrored
monograms from a negative matrix determinant, hover-only text that never engaged because
its media query is false in a headless browser, a two-alphabet monogram from mixed-script
titles, and two verification rounds that read a stale deploy.
